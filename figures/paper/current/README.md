Localization plots use the atlas population of 20,333 human records of at least
50 residues. Pair rankings use all 20,431 canonical human records and 2,222 viral
records. Listed pairs require ≥10% coverage of either sequence before rounding;
the selected views contain 32,686 eligible pairs at ≥7/9 and 76 at 9/9. Each
coverage denominator is that sequence's full length. Ties use opposite-end
coverage, then accessions. Displayed percentages retain native one-decimal
precision.

The SVGs include export metadata. The complete figure archive on Zenodo
also contains genomic-position views and other layouts available on the website.
