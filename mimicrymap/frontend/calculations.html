How Mimicry Results
Are Calculated

Exact matching is used to find and map linear molecular mimic motifs across proteomes.

  1. Select Records

    Choose one target record and the parent records to compare with it.

  2. Extract Window Pairs

    Take every nine-residue window from each record and align across all-possible target and parent pairs.

  3. Apply Core Threshold

    Retain window pairs with at least the required number of identical positions.

  4. Map Context

    Locate each qualifying pair in the original records and extend computation to the surrounding sequence.

  5. Inspect and Summarize Results

    Inspect alignments and sequence coverage; export summary data for enrichment analysis.

The full method is below, walked through for an important example: discovery of the 5/9 molecular mimic core XSPPRXPXX shared between EBNA1 (from Epstein-Barr virus) and the human neuronal protein HEPACAM (also called GlialCAM). Aberrant immunity targeting this site is found in about 1/4 of multiple sclerosis patients and may be a cause of multiple sclerosis. As we will see, 5/9 mimicry events to proteins like HEPACAM are commonly found across human viruses.
