Data Sources, Methods,
and Licenses

Download sequence maps for canonical human proteins, exposure proteins and assay libraries. Compare your own FASTAs on the MimicryMap homepage, or download the local pipeline.

Each coverage-summary ZIP contains one CSV per direction, or one CSV for a self-comparison. Tables retain target identifiers and sequences, with pair-specific coverage and matched residue ranges at 7/9, 8/9 and 9/9. Coverage uses each sequence’s full length; listed pairs cover at least 10% of either sequence. Unmatched Target Regions describe residues without a qualifying match anywhere in the stated comparison library, including matches below the pair-list cutoff. Download the field and methods guide.

Canonical Sequence Maps and Research Downloads

Start with canonical protein maps: reference-to-reference and assay-to-reference comparisons. Direct cross-assay summaries are available below for research. MimicryMap calculates full sequence maps on demand.

MimicryMap Dataset on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0. Supplemental downloads include the pan-viral atlas data and figures, input FASTAs, structures, residue annotations and readers.

General-Use Summaries (.csv)
Directional CSVs of record pairs, coverage, matched ranges and unmatched target regions, with the original input FASTAs and a short README.
Mapping Database (.sqlite)
A queryable SQLite database of sequence relationships and residue coverage in both directions, from the same comparisons.

Mapping databases contain sequence evidence; antibody measurements and enrichment results are calculated separately. Open coverage summaries as CSVs or query the mapping databases with SQLite.

References, Data Sources and Licenses

  • Assay libraries: HuScan, full VirScan with the Exposome add-on, and HuProt 4.0 are assays run by CDI Labs. These libraries retain the assayed antigen sequences and their original identifiers.
  • Biological annotations and predicted tracks: UniProt supplies protein descriptions, genes, subcellular locations, topology, modifications and HumSaVar variants. Gene Ontology cellular-component annotations provide a location fallback where UniProt has no classifiable location. AlphaFold supplies predicted structures and confidence scores; FreeSASA supplies structure-derived solvent accessibility, and pepsickle supplies predicted proteasomal-cleavage tracks.
